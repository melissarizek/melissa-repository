Frequently asked questions

How does the Swag Store work?

Visit go/swagstore, select the items you'd like to purchase, add to cart, and check out.

How long will it take to receive my newly ordered swag?

You can expect to receive your swag within one week of submitting your order.

Will I receive a tracking number once I've placed my order?

Yes — you can expect to receive a tracking number shortly after placing your order.

Can I buy gifts for friends and family?

Yes! Please feel free to buy swag for your friends and family. In fact, you can also share the URL to our swag store with them.

I've seen Upstarters wearing swag that's not offered in the store. Where do I get those items?

Those items might be specific to anniversaries, team swag, or legacy swag. While we may bring back legacy swag, anniversary swag will be limited to your work anniversaries.

How do I order swag for my team?

For teams that would like to order team swag, please visit go/team-swag to get started.
