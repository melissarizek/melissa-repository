Ordering Team Swag: The Full Process

Follow these steps to order swag through our partner, Brilliant. Plan ahead — the full process typically takes 4–6 weeks.

💡

Budget guidance for onsites: Total budget is $200/person. Swag is recommended at $25–$40/person. Align with your VP and EA team before ordering. Minimum order quantity is typically 25 units.

1
Get Budget Approval
Align with your Director before proceeding — and your VP as well, in some cases.
  • Confirm swag fits within the overall onsite per-person budget
  • Coordinate with your EA team if applicable
2
Browse & Select Swag
Review Brilliant's lookbook for current options. Add your item to your cart, confirm quantity, and enter shipping info to generate an estimate.
Custom design? If your idea isn't in the lookbook, submit a request through the Creative Design Service Desk before proceeding. Brand approval is required, and custom artwork may add cost.
3
Initiate Order with Brilliant
Email upstart@brilliantmade.com to kick off your order. Include:
  • Project overview (audience, goals, gifting purpose)
  • Budget, quantity, and sizes (if applicable)
  • Delivery date and delivery address(es)
  • If shipping to individuals: gather recipient addresses using this template and confirm mailing consent
4
Review Your Order
Brilliant will reach out to confirm your order and include a link to review and approve or edit your artwork.
5
Get Procurement Approval via Zip Required
The approval path depends on your quote amount:
Quote > $1,000
Submit a Zip Fastrack ticket and attach the quote. Zip is first-come, first-served — submit early.

Questions: procurement@upstart.com
Quote < $1,000
No Zip ticket needed. Get VP approval via email, then send the invoice & approval to bills@upstart.com.
How to submit a Zip Fastrack ticket — go to upstart.zip.com → New Request → Fastrack Purchase Request (Limited Categories)
Page 1
General Information
Check the acknowledgement box · Select Yes for contract/proposal received · Search category: Swag · Request type: New Contract · Department: select your department · Subsidiary: Upstart Network, Inc. or Upstart Mortgage · Name the request (e.g. "Swag for team event") · Add a brief description of the purchase
Page 2
Vendor Details
Search for Brilliant — they're already a preferred vendor · Select your Brilliant contact (e.g. Claire Morrison, Senior Account Manager) · San Mateo office access: No
Page 3
Contract Details
Enter estimated total value (note: Fastrack cannot be used for contracts over $200K) · Payment type: One-time payment · Enter service start and end dates · Set contract signature needed-by date
Page 4
Documents
Upload the vendor quote/proposal (required) · Attach any additional reference files · Click Submit request
Reference the Procurement Policy for full details.
6
Approve Order in Brilliant
Once procurement approves, go to your Brilliant dashboard, select your estimate, and click Approve.
Payment always goes through AP — never via credit card. Brilliant will contact the AP team directly. All invoices must be sent to bills@upstart.com.
7
Production & Shipping 🎉
Brilliant begins production once payment is confirmed and ships directly to your submitted address(es). Brilliant's inventory isn't guaranteed until the estimate is approved, so don't delay.
